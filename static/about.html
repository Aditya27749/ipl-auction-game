About T20 Auction Simulator

T20 Auction Simulator is a passion project built for cricket fans who love the strategic element of franchise building. While most fantasy sports focus on predicting player performance, we focus on the intense psychological warfare of the auction room.

Our custom-built AI engine calculates over 50 different variables to accurately score your drafted team based on structural balance, raw batting firepower, and economic bowling. We built this game to settle the debate once and for all: Who among your friends is the ultimate cricket strategist?

Enjoy the bidding wars, manage your budget, and may the best franchise win!
