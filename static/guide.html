Official Strategy Guide

Welcome to the official T20 Cricket Auction Strategy Guide. If you want to dominate your friends and consistently score a perfect 10.0 from our AI Evaluator, you need to master the art of squad composition and budget management. This guide will walk you through the core mechanics of the simulation.

1. The Mathematics of a Champion Squad

In our simulator, money alone does not buy victory. The AI Evaluator grades your final roster based on a strict set of rules. You must draft exactly 15 players across four specific roles:

  • 5 Batsmen: Look for players with high career strike rates.
  • 3 All-Rounders: These are the most valuable players in the game, as they contribute to both runs and wickets.
  • 5 Bowlers: Focus on economy. Wicket-takers are great, but economical bowlers prevent negative score penalties.
  • 2 Wicket Keepers: A scarce resource. Secure them early.
⚠️ THE OVERSEAS PENALTY: The Golden Rule of franchise cricket applies here. You may only draft a maximum of 6 overseas players. Drafting a 7th overseas player will trigger an immediate and irreversible mathematical deduction on your final score.

2. Managing Your Strike Rate & Economy

The AI does not just look at total runs; it calculates efficiency. To win, your squad must meet two aggressive thresholds:

The Strike Rate Rule (>= 137.0)

Your team's combined average batting strike rate must stay above 137.0. If you draft slow, anchoring batsmen, you must balance them out with explosive lower-order hitters like Andre Russell or Kieron Pollard. Falling below 137.0 results in a massive 20.0 point penalty before division.

The Economy Rule (<= 7.70)

T20 cricket is a batsman's game, but economy wins championships. Your bowling attack must maintain a combined average economy rate below 7.70. Avoid fast bowlers who leak runs, and prioritize mystery spinners who can choke the run flow in the middle overs.

3. The Secret Mission Mechanic

Before every auction, the AI secretly assigns each player a "Secret Captain." This is usually a high-profile superstar. If you fail to draft your assigned Secret Captain by the end of the auction, your final AI rating will suffer a permanent -0.5 deduction. You must decide whether to overpay for your target or take the penalty and build a better overall squad.
